# User Interviews

## Interview 1
**Name:** Abdullah Ahmed
**Role & Stage:** Former Intern at Yuga Yatra Retail / CS Student
**Duration:** 12 minutes (WhatsApp call)

**Direct Quotes:**
1. "Bro, I don't even track it. I pay for ChatGPT Plus and just pray my card doesn't decline at the end of the month."
2. "I wouldn't cancel Copilot even if it's redundant with Cursor because setting up the SSH keys and extensions again is too much of a headache."
3. "If some random website told me I was wasting $40, I'd assume it was a scam trying to steal my OpenAI API key."

**The most surprising thing they said:**
I assumed the primary motivator would be saving money, but Abdullah explicitly stated he would rather lose $20/month than spend an hour migrating his workflow and IDE settings. Convenience severely outweighed cost.

**What it changed about my design:**
His distrust of "random calculators" made me realize I couldn't just throw numbers on a screen. I added the detailed FAQ section to the landing page specifically to explain *how* the math works locally and explicitly state that we don't need their billing info or API keys to run the audit.
