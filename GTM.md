# Go-To-Market Strategy: Credex Audit

## 1. The Exact Target User
We are not targeting "startups." We are targeting **Fractional CFOs** and **VP of Engineering / Head of Engineering** at Series A and Series B startups (approx. 50–200 employees). 

At this stage, a company has enough developers that overlapping AI seat licenses (e.g., paying for both GitHub Copilot and Cursor) creates a massive, bleeding hole in the budget. However, they are not quite large enough to have a dedicated software procurement team, meaning the responsibility of auditing this spend usually falls on an overworked VP of Eng or a contracted Fractional CFO.

## 2. The Trigger
They don't wake up wanting an "AI Audit." The trigger event happens when they receive a monthly automated alert from Brex or Ramp notifying them that their "Software Subscriptions" category has increased by 15% month-over-month. Alternatively, the trigger is the week before a quarterly board meeting when they are aggressively looking for operational fat to trim to extend their runway.

## 3. Where They Hang Out
- **Private Slack Communities:** "Fractional Executive Network", "CTOs in Tech", and the "B2B SaaS Founders" channels.
- **Newsletters:** Readers of "Pragmatic Engineer" (Gergely Orosz) or specific subreddits like `r/ExperiencedDevs` and `r/SaaS`.
- **Twitter/X:** Lurking in threads complaining about OpenAI API costs or Vercel billing surprises.

## 4. Getting the First 100 Users ($0 Budget)
I will bypass standard social media shouting and use a "Channel Partner" hustle strategy. 

1. **The Fractional CFO Trojan Horse:** I will LinkedIn DM 50 Fractional CFOs who service Series A startups. *The Pitch:* "I built a free tool that instantly identifies AI subscription waste. Run your clients' tech stacks through it, find $1,000/mo in savings, and present it to them to look like a hero. No strings attached." Since their job is to save clients money to justify their own retainer, they will become our primary distribution engine.
2. **The "Roast My Stack" Thread:** I will post in the `r/SaaS` and `r/startup` subreddits, not with a link to the tool, but with a challenge: *"Drop your team size and AI tools in the comments. I will reply with exactly how much money you are wasting and how to fix it."* I will run their inputs through the Credex Audit tool manually and reply with the personalized AI summary and a screenshot of the dashboard. The sheer value of the replies will drive organic curiosity and clicks to the tool's URL in my bio.

## 5. The Unfair Distribution Channel
The unfair advantage is **Credex's existing inventory of discounted enterprise credits**. Anyone can build a basic calculator that tells a founder they are spending too much. But only Credex can diagnose the disease *and immediately provide the cure*. Our unfair channel is the "One-Click Fix." The audit doesn't just say "downgrade to save $500"; it says "Click here to claim a Credex Enterprise partnership and save $500 right now."

## 6. Week-1 Traction Expectations
If the Fractional CFO outreach and the "Roast" threads hit, a successful Week 1 looks like this:
- **Top of Funnel:** 400 Unique Visitors to the landing page.
- **Activation:** 120 completed audits (30% conversion rate from the landing page, aided by the "No Login Required" frictionless design).
- **Lead Capture:** 15 high-intent emails captured from users who were flagged as having >$500/mo in potential savings.
- **Bottom of Funnel:** 2 Credex consultations booked from the automated Resend email drip.
